# Case 03 — AI-Enabled Network Product

**Role:** Technical Product Owner  
**Domain:** Network / AI-enabled Products / Observability  
**Environment:** Enterprise Technology

## Context

Within a network and telecom product environment, initiatives were being developed around AI agents and automation. These initiatives depended on specialized AI, observability and AIOps capabilities.

The Product Owner's responsibility was to connect the product problem with the technical ecosystem required to deliver and evolve the solution.

## Product challenge

AI-enabled products introduce an additional layer of complexity. Beyond defining a feature, the Product Owner needs to understand expected behavior, integrations, dependencies, quality expectations, operational constraints and how the product will evolve after initial delivery.

## Product discovery approach

My starting point for an AI product is a structured "product bath" — first understanding the product before trying to manage its delivery.

I look at:

1. What the product is intended to solve.
2. Who uses it and who owns its outcomes.
3. What the product is responsible for.
4. How it is supported and evolved.
5. Which systems and teams it depends on.
6. What behavior and outputs are expected.
7. What constraints, risks and governance requirements apply.
8. How success should be observed and measured.

This creates enough product understanding to lead discovery and prioritization without pretending to be the specialist responsible for every AI component.

## AI-agent context

The initiatives involved agents interacting with other technical capabilities to support network and operational use cases.

Specialized teams owned the deeper AI, observability and AIOps implementation. My Product Owner role focused on:

- Product problem and expected outcome
- Scope and responsibilities
- User and stakeholder needs
- Integration and dependency mapping
- Backlog structure
- Prioritization
- Delivery coordination
- Evolution of the product after implementation

## Product lifecycle

For an AI-enabled technical product, I consider the lifecycle as:

**Discovery → Definition → Delivery → Adoption → Monitoring → Learning → Evolution**

This prevents the product from being treated as complete merely because an AI agent has been implemented.

## Technical product perspective

My infrastructure and network background provides useful context for AI initiatives because the agent operates within a larger technical ecosystem: networks, cloud services, infrastructure, observability, security, APIs and operational processes.

The Product Owner needs to understand these relationships sufficiently to ask the right questions and make dependencies visible.

## Key takeaway

The value of a Technical Product Owner in AI is not claiming to be the AI engineer. It is the ability to understand the product deeply enough to connect the user problem, expected behavior, technical dependencies, delivery constraints and continuous evolution.
