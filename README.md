## How I work as a Technical Product Owner

1. Understand the product, problem and expected outcome.
2. Identify users, stakeholders, dependencies, constraints and operational impact.
3. Translate complex technical needs into structured product initiatives.
4. Refine scope with engineering and technical leadership.
5. Prioritize according to value, risk, dependencies, urgency and capacity.
6. Maintain roadmap and backlog visibility.
7. Track delivery, changes and impediments.
8. Validate outcomes and feed learning back into product evolution.
